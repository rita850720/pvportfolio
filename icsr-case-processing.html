ICSR case processing

From published case report to structured safety case

Three literature cases demonstrate case-validity assessment, clinically coherent data extraction, seriousness and expectedness evaluation, narrative writing, documented follow-up needs, and quality control.

Documented workflow

Case-processing path

Each example makes the reasoning traceable from source identification through the final case disposition.

01Literature source

Identify and document the source article.

02Validity assessment

Confirm patient, reporter, product, and event.

03Data extraction

Capture only information supported by the source.

04Medical coding

Assign defensible event concepts and terminology.

05Medical assessment

Evaluate seriousness, expectedness, and causality.

06Case narrative

Synthesize chronology without adding assumptions.

07Reporting decision

Document the regulatory rationale and disposition.

08Quality control

Reconcile source, fields, narrative, and assessment.
