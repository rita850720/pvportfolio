Literature-specific reproducibility

Literature surveillance methodology

This workflow is distinct from the openFDA spontaneous-report method used in the Safety Surveillance section. Its unit of review is a bibliographic publication, followed—when applicable—by one or more identifiable patients described in that publication.

End-to-end workflow

Define product, database, and date window
Build broad and case-sensitive searches
Deduplicate and screen every record
Locate lawful full-text access
Assess minimum ICSR criteria
Evaluate seriousness and expectedness
Route case and aggregate findings
Reconcile QC and issue the report

Search and screening

Product scope includes the proprietary name, generic or proper name, relevant development identifiers, and product-family names where appropriate. The broad PubMed search is paired with a case-report and case-series sensitivity strategy. Titles and abstracts are screened against documented inclusion and exclusion rules, and each record receives a disposition.

Full-text and case assessment

Publicly and lawfully accessible full text is reviewed when available; otherwise, conclusions are limited to the title, abstract, and metadata. Candidate cases are assessed for the four minimum ICSR elements: an identifiable patient, an identifiable reporter, a suspect or interacting medicinal product, and an adverse event or other reportable observation. Seriousness, expectedness, causality information, confounding, dechallenge or rechallenge, special situations, and duplicate potential are evaluated separately.

Aggregate-safety assessment

Publications that do not support an individual ICSR may still inform signal detection, known-risk characterization, benefit–risk evaluation, or periodic aggregate reporting. Clinical trials, cohorts, registries, pharmacovigilance analyses, exposure studies, and therapeutic-use cases are therefore retained when they contain product-relevant safety evidence.

Default review framework

  • Jurisdiction: United States / FDA.
  • Expectedness reference: current FDA-approved U.S. Prescribing Information available at the time of review.
  • Individual-case framework: ICH E2D(R1), supplemented by applicable FDA postmarketing requirements.
  • Aggregate relevance: ICH E2C(R2) principles, applied as appropriate to the evidence.
  • Override: another jurisdiction or reference safety information may be specified for a particular project.

Reproducibility package

Each final report retains the exact strategy and dates, screening reconciliation, full-text access status, per-publication review basis, case-level rationale when applicable, excluded-record log, limitations, references, and QC status. The reports use only public information and do not contain confidential employer, patient, or unpublished program information.
