Work in progress

Literature surveillance

A companion portfolio stream for documenting repeatable literature searches, safety screening, case-validity assessment, aggregate-safety relevance, and quality control.

Planned workflow

Define product scope and search period
Build database-specific search strings
Screen titles and abstracts
Review full text and duplicates
Assess minimum case criteria
Evaluate expectedness and reportability
Identify signal or aggregate relevance
Record QC and final disposition

Core surveillance log fields

Field groupExamples
Search controlDatabase, query, date range, run date, reviewer
CitationTitle, authors, journal, year, DOI or PMID
ScreeningDuplicate, in scope, full text available, exclusion reason
Case relevanceIdentifiable patient, reporter, suspect product, adverse event
Safety assessmentSeriousness, expectedness, causality information, special situation
Aggregate relevanceNew risk information, known-risk characterization, benefit-risk or PBRER relevance
DispositionICSR candidate, aggregate-only, noncase safety information, not relevant
Quality controlSecond review, reconciliation, decision rationale, completion date

Portfolio development plan

The first published example will include a transparent search protocol, a sanitized screening log, selected decision rationales, and a short surveillance summary. No confidential employer, patient, or unpublished program information will be used.

Status: Framework established; example literature-surveillance logs are being prepared.
