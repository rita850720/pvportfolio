Analytical approach

Designed for transparent interpretation

The work emphasizes reproducibility and data provenance while keeping spontaneous-report limitations visible.

FDA-grounded sourcing

Current labels, regulatory histories, safety communications, pediatric reviews, and openFDA data.

Multi-name retrieval

Brand, generic, proper, active-substance, presentation, and biosimilar terms are tested across relevant fields.

Label-based AESIs

Prespecified exact-PT groups support screening while remaining distinct from validated SMQs or adjudicated cases.

Bounded conclusions

Counts are treated as hypothesis-generating evidence, not incidence estimates, comparative risk, or proof of causality.
