Professional background

Translational science applied to drug safety

I bring a research foundation in immunology and inflammation together with hands-on adverse-event investigation, literature synthesis, clinical-safety reasoning, and independent pharmacovigilance training.

Profile

I am a translational scientist with more than three years of biotechnology experience and a PhD in Translational Biology, Medicine, and Health. My work has included reverse-translational investigation of adverse events observed in Phase 1 studies, cross-functional safety evaluation, IND-enabling support, mechanism-of-action research, and integration of clinical and nonclinical evidence.

I developed this portfolio to demonstrate how I approach pharmacovigilance questions: define the product and regulatory context, make search logic reproducible, separate observed data from interpretation, and keep the limits of each evidence source explicit.

Pharmacovigilance capabilities

  • ICSR intake, validation, duplicate search, data entry, and narrative writing
  • MedDRA coding and structured adverse-event assessment
  • Seriousness, causality, expectedness, and reportability concepts
  • Oracle Argus Safety training and case-processing workflow
  • Literature surveillance and safety-information triage
  • FDA labeling, safety communications, and openFDA FAERS/AEMS research
  • Cross-functional communication across clinical, toxicology, bioinformatics, and regulatory functions

Scientific foundation

My doctoral research focused on innate immune mechanisms in sepsis and inflammatory disease. I have authored six peer-reviewed publications and hold medical laboratory and professional coding credentials, with additional training in Good Clinical Practice and pharmacovigilance.
