Otezla safety surveillance

A postauthorization review of apremilast and Otezla product names integrating the current U.S. label, application history, FDA surveillance checks, and aggregate openFDA FAERS/AEMS counts.

Surveillance question

Which leading PTs and label-based AESI screens emerge from a comprehensive apremilast name search, and how should high-volume findings be interpreted against the current label?

Product and label context

Otezla contains apremilast. The current prescribing information covers immediate-release Otezla and Otezla XR. Label safety topics include severe gastrointestinal reactions, depression including suicidal thoughts or behavior, weight decrease, hypersensitivity, and interactions with strong CYP450 enzyme inducers.

Multi-name search

The cohort tested OTEZLA, OTEZLA XR, APREMILAST, and the development identifier CC-10004 across reported medicinal-product, active-substance, normalized generic, and normalized brand fields. Zero-match Otezla XR terms were retained for reproducible future updates.

Interpretation

Gastrointestinal PTs, headache, depression-related terms, weight and appetite terms, and hypersensitivity concepts were broadly concordant with current labeling. Psoriasis, psoriatic arthropathy, arthralgia, and joint swelling may reflect approved indications or disease course. Drug ineffective, therapy non-responder, condition aggravated, and dose-omission terms describe effectiveness or use circumstances and should not automatically be treated as adverse pharmacology.
