Tepezza safety surveillance

A postauthorization review of teprotumumab-trbw integrating U.S. labeling, FDA regulatory history, AEMS surveillance context, and a multi-name openFDA FAERS/AEMS cohort.

Surveillance question

How do the leading reported PTs and prespecified adverse events of special interest align with the current U.S. label, and what additional FDA surveillance topics warrant monitoring?

Product and label context

Tepezza is the proprietary name for teprotumumab-trbw. The report identified seven FDA-approved postauthorization labeling supplements through 24 November 2025. The current label warns about infusion reactions, inflammatory bowel disease, hyperglycemia, and hearing impairment, including hearing loss that may be severe and permanent.

Multi-name search

The cohort included exact reported product terms TEPEZZA, TEPROTUMUMAB, and TEPROTUMUMAB-TRBW, plus active-substance and normalized generic-name fields. The individual searches overlapped and were combined with OR logic rather than summed.

Interpretation

Muscle spasms, fatigue, nausea, diarrhea, alopecia, headache, and weight decrease were consistent with labeled common reactions. Tinnitus, hypoacusis, deafness, and other hearing PTs aligned with the hearing-impairment warning, while increased glucose and diabetes-related terms aligned with hyperglycemia labeling. Hypertension, encephalopathy, and hypersensitivity were tracked separately as FDA potential-signal topics under evaluation rather than established causal reactions.
